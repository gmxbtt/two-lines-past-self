Two lines sent back to a past self: a warning about one specific moment — a 2 a.m. text that should never have been sent — and why that moment mattered. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Two Lines to Your Past Self" bounty, $2 in USDC.

> Don't send that 2 a.m. text to her — you already know the answer, and she already knows you know.
> The silence after it cost you three years of friendship you never got back.

Line one names one concrete moment; line two traces what changed because of it — no general life lesson, no wasted words.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (173 characters, two lines, UTF-8).
